The post title seems very abstract for a second post of mine. You may wonder that I've gone crazy to write my thoughts on that particular object. Though, it's one of the most recurrent pattern I witness every single day of my life.

If you type the word "chain" on a search browser or open a dictionary, you will find among the common definitions something similar to:

> a series of things linked, connected, or associated together

This looks also very abstract, and most people thinks it's just obvious, then, stop there. Sure, everything in this world interacts structurally with each others. That's why I'll not question here its nature or reinvent another *Great chain of being* and call it a day.

## From the hatred chain...

This chain is the most easiest one I could find these days. It's on my phone, on TV news, sometimes among social interactions... And the worst part, aside from being highly corrosive, is its cyclic and poisonous nature. It starts little from a misconception, propagates as fast as fire, trying to destroys everything, no matter what, until it ends temporarly, before restarting because of a sparkle.

Luckyly enough, you can break free from it by using as soon as possible your self-awareness. Once you know that you are part of a hatred chain that is profitable to the upper links, then you realize there is no point of being such a link, except if your highly motivated by your own hatred. It's really just about staying or leaving a toxic environment.

## ...to the inspiration chain

This one is becoming more and more rare. It's hidden in some books, in art, in the nature... The reason why some humans fall in love with art, above its beauty, is because there will be always a suprising perspective to find somewhere, questioning our superstitious nature and upgrading our technics.

4 years ago, I've been mentored by someone that pointed me some intereseting resources about an artsy approach to programming leading to craft smaller and more performant code. He gave me generously articles and videos created by game developers like Jonathan Blow and the **LEGENDARY** Casey Muratori, but also inviting me in the [Handmade Network](https://handmade.network/) community, that originated as a gathering of fans of Casey's [Handmade Hero](https://guide.handmadehero.org/) video series. Since, I've learnt to dodge most of idiocratic industrial dogmas that could've made my programming journey worse.

# Conclusion

Inspiration and hatred are social chains. One is constructive, one is destructive. What's making its state mutating is up to the trend of its links, moduled by the strength of its motivations. It's up to your independant and critical thinking to contribute, fight against or leave it.

Some technical posts may arise somedays, if I manage to be done with my R&D on the state of art of UI renderering profitting to my upcoming software...

I wish you all to find a robust enough inspirational chain if it's not already the case, or maybe an opportunity to create a new one !
Bye now.
