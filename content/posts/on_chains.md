The post title seems very abstract for a second post of mine. You may wonder that I've gone crazy to write my thoughts on that particular object. Though, it's one of the most recurrent pattern I witness every single day of my life.

If you type the word "chain" on a search browser or open a dictionary, you will find among the common definitions something similar to:

> a series of things linked, connected, or associated together

This looks also very abstract, and most people thinks it's just obvious, then, stop there. Sure, everything in this world interacts structurally with each others. That's why I'll not question here its nature or reinventing another *Great chain of being* and call it a day. I will focus on two I want to share some spiritual thoughts.

## From the hatred chain...

This chain is the most easiest one I could find these days. It's on my phone, on TV news, sometimes among social interactions... And the worst part, aside from being highly corrosive, is its cyclic and poisonous nature. It starts little from a misconception, propagates as fast as fire, trying to destroys everything, no matter what, until it ends temporarly, before restarting because of a sparkle.

Luckyly enough, that chain is fragile most of the time, and you can break free from it by using your self-awareness and critical thinking as soon as possible. Once you know that you are part of a hatred chain that is profitable to the upper links, then you realize there is no point of being such a link. That doesn't mean you will escape every single ones tho. It could be harder to escape when the environment is becoming too violent.

## ...to the inspiration chain

This smaller one is becoming more and more rare. It's hidden in some books, in art, within communities... The reason why some humans fall in love with art, above its beauty, is because there will be always a suprising perspective to find somewhere, questioning our superstitious nature and upgrading our technics.

4 years ago, I've been mentored by someone that pointed me some intereseting resources about an artsy approach to programming leading to craft smaller and more performant code. He gave me generously articles and videos created by game developers like Jonathan Blow and the **LEGENDARY** Casey Muratori, but also inviting me in the [Handmade Network](https://handmade.network/) community, that originated as a gathering of folks that liked Casey's [Handmade Hero](https://guide.handmadehero.org/) video series. Since, I've learnt to dodge most of industrial dogmas that could have made my programming journey worse.

## Beware of dependencies

I believe hatred and inspiration share something in common. Both are chains. It always start from a single link and expands.
What differentiate them is the trend of its links. Hatred links would morph as a flail, insipiration ones would morph an asymetrical spider web. One is destructive, one is constructive.

I like to represent hatred as a flail because it starts from few links that becomes bad and then hook up with a lot of links that never really communicates each others and agree blindly to the first link's vision, so to everyone else. So because one link would hook a lot of others, you would endup as a ball.

I also noticed that eventually, some inspirational chains could become destructive depending on how its growth is protected from bad actors. I never managed a community at the scale of the Handmade Network, but I noticed that it takes few links discussing without holding anything back, sharing their knowledge and opinions and even sometimes working together to reinvent a more solid wheel to make the place inpisrational enough.

The title of that section is an invitation to expand your independent critical thiking, because anyone could easily faint to be inspirational but gatekeeping the essential of a set of truths to make you dependent to their visions. We usually call them as gurus, but really, it could be anyone. One of the key is to take the necessary time for exploring yourself what you consider important __essentially__.
